About This Reel

THE WORLD IS A GHETTO is a film treatment by Isaiah Glick, set in South Los Angeles between 1969 and 1979. The title borrows from WAR's 1972 album of the same name — the record scoring the quick-cut test reel on the home page.

Reel 1 is a rapid-cut test montage built from period stills and single-word beats drawn from the treatment's own vocabulary. Reel 2 is the treatment itself, still being written — the version on this site reflects the outline as it currently stands.
