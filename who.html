Who It Is

Co-Founder, the Crips

Raymond Washington

One of the co-founders of the Crips, determined to stamp out the petty thieves and gangsters terrorizing the people of South Central. While he is initially successful, his efforts are undone by the petty ambitions and rivalries, leading to full-on gang war. He ultimately falls victim to those forces he sought to control in his assassination.

  • Tookie Williams

    One of the co-founders of the Crips, an amateur bodybuilder who shares Washington’s ambitions but, when his friend is sent to prison, cannot keep the gang together. He becomes addicted to drugs and loses his job, forcing him to rob banks in desperation. Moreover, he defies his old friend’s insistence on not using guns, which leads Washington to turn him in with the police.

  • Mac Thomas

    The third member of the founding troika of the Crips, who is mysteriously murdered around the time Washington is sent to prison.

  • Buddha Morrow

    Tookie’s best friend and a key enforcer in the Crips, whose death leads Washington and Williams to declare full-on war against the Bloods.
