Treatment

The man who built the most dangerous gang in American history was murdered by it.

In 1969, Raymond Washington, a young and ambitious teenager in South Central Los Angeles, decides to form a gang, the Baby Avenues, in South Central Los Angeles, inspired by the example of the local Black Panthers. He sees an opportunity to dominate the criminal underworld, and they start expanding through the neighborhood. Washington’s vision is for more than just a gang, wanting to protect the Black community from police harassment and random street violence.

In 1971, he comes into contact with Tookie Williams, an aspiring bodybuilder just out of juvie who leads his own gang, and Mac Thomas, a Compton-based gang leader. Together, Washington, Williams, and Thomas form the Crips, the name stemming from their habit of carrying around canes. The members of the gang start fanning out across the south side of Los Angeles and challenging local gang leaders to fight to compel them to join their larger organization. Within a year, the Crips have become the dominant gang, but their growing power incurs the anger of many of the rival gangs who worry about them getting too powerful. Among them are Sylvester Scott and Benson Owens, who were attacked by Washington’s old gang when they were all in high school.

1972 · the Wilson Pickett concert

After a teenager is murdered by a group of Crips at a Wilson Pickett concert in 1972, Scott and Owens join their gangs into the Bloods and declare war. They kill Tookie’s best friend Buddha Morrow, leading the Crips to adopt the blue bandana as their uniform in his honor.

Raymond Washington

The war continues through the streets of Los Angeles, and amidst the fighting Mac Thomas is mysteriously murdered and Washington is arrested for armed robbery and sent to prison in 1974. Tookie leads the gang in his stead, but without his friend’s leadership the organization devolves into infighting, drug dealing, and even using guns which Washington deplored.

In 1976, he is shot in a drive-by shooting by the Bloods and becomes addicted to PCP. The next year, Washington is paroled from prison to find the Crips in shambles and Tookie effectively incapacitated. He decides to reunite the warring sets in the hopes of getting them to negotiate a truce and end the gang war once and for all. However, at this point, the violence had spiraled beyond his control.

Meanwhile, Tookie, desperate for money now that he lost his job as an anti-gang guidance counselor, turns to armed robbery. In early 1979, he robs a 7/11 convenience store and a nearby motel, killing both the store clerk and the family who owned the motel. Raymond, who warned Tookie not to go through with the murders, anonymously sends evidence to the police that his erstwhile friend was involved, and Tookie is soon arrested and sent to jail. From behind bars, he convinces his Crip allies to shoot Raymond dead. The movie closes with a drive-by shooting, as Raymond is killed by fellow members of the gang that he created.
