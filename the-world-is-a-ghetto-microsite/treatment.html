The Treatment — South Los Angeles, 1969–1979

Reel 02 · South Los Angeles, 1969–1979

REEL 02 · 01/08
TREATMENT
1969

01 / 08 — 1969

1969

Raymond Washington forms a crew in South Los Angeles — the Baby Avenues.

02 / 08 — 1971

1971

Washington joins forces with Tookie Williams and Mac Thomas. Together they form the Crips and begin taking over the Los Angeles criminal underworld.

03 / 08 — 1972

1972

A murder at a Wilson Pickett concert — Robert Ballou, shot dead — catalyzes the rival gangs of Los Angeles to organize. They call themselves the Bloods.

04 / 08 — 1973

1973

The Bloods kill Buddha Morrow. What was a rivalry becomes a war.

05 / 08 — 1974

1974

Washington is arrested for armed robbery and sent to jail. Tookie takes over leadership of the Crips in his place.

06 / 08 — 1976

1976

Washington returns from jail to a gang wracked by infighting — and to Tookie, injured and addicted to PCP.

07 / 08 — 1977

1977

Washington tries to pull the gang back under his leadership and broker a truce with the Bloods. He fails.

08 / 08 — 1979

1979

Tookie is arrested on homicide charges. Before the year is out, Washington is dead — killed in a drive-by.
