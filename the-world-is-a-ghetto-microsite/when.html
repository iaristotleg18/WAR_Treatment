When It Is

1969–1979

Takes place from 1969 to 1979, but focused around the 1971–1974 period with an extended coda in 1979 when everything falls apart. The promise of liberation of the 1960s falls to the mundane realities and petty squabbles of the late 1970s. By the end of the film, the long arm of the law as embodied by LAPD Commissioner Daryl Gates has permanently entrenched itself.
