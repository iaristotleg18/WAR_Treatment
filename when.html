WHEN IT IS

Temporal context of the montage

Mid‑Century America

The source material originates from the 1950s — a period defined by domestic optimism, product innovation, and the rise of consumer advertising. The montage captures this era’s visual language and reinterprets it through contemporary motion design.

A Modern Reframing

Although the imagery is historical, the experience is modern. The sequencing, pacing, and cinematic grid place the archive in a present‑day editorial context, creating a dialogue between past and present.
